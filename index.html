What We Do

Capabilities

We build systems that reason causally — not statistically. The same engine operates in two modes depending on what the application demands.

Constrained Mode — Regulated Industries

Deterministic, auditable reasoning for healthcare, finance, legal, and security. A confidential reasoning engine (USPTO provisional filed) produces identical output on every run with full traceability. Not prompt engineering — structural. Every inference is reviewable, every conclusion traceable.

Emergent, human-like reasoning for games, virtual worlds, and robotics. NPCs that think and act like real people because nothing is hardcoded — they reason causally about their environment. You have no idea how they will attack, defend, or cooperate. It is like playing a real person.
